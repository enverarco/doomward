Doomward Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Doomward ("the app") is a walking game. This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it. In short: your data stays on your phone. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

Information the app uses

  • Step counts. On iPhone, the app reads your step count from the device's motion sensor (Apple Core Motion). On Android, it reads daily step totals from Health Connect, or from the phone's step sensor while the app is open. Only step counts are read, and only to calculate your progress in the game.
  • Things you enter. Your hero's name, preferred units, stride length, daily goal, reminder time, and any walks you log by hand.

Where it is stored

All of the above is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. The app has no user accounts and no servers, and it does not send your data anywhere. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software.

Health Connect (Android)

The app requests read-only access to Steps and, optionally, permission to read in the background so your journey can update and notify you while the app is closed. Data obtained through Health Connect is used solely to show your progress in the game. It is never transferred, sold, used for advertising, or used to determine credit-worthiness or for lending purposes. The app's use of this information complies with the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time in the Health Connect app.

Sound

Optional sound effects (footsteps and ambient sounds) are played from files built into the app. The app does not use or request access to the microphone.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local notifications on your device (landmark arrivals and an optional daily reminder). No push notification service or server is involved.

Sharing

If you tap "Share my progress", the app opens your phone's standard share sheet with a short text message. Nothing is shared unless you choose a destination and send it.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Start a new journey to erase your progress, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
