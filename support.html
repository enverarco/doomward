Doomward Support

Doomward: Walking Quest, for iPhone and Android

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email oisinmacsweeney@hotmail.com. Please say which phone you're using and what you were doing when the problem happened.

My steps aren't counting

iPhone: open Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness. Make sure Fitness Tracking is on and Doomward is allowed. Steps from the last 7 days are picked up the next time you open the app.

Android: Doomward reads steps from Health Connect. Open Doomward's Settings → Open Health Connect and check that Doomward may read Steps. Some phones need an app that writes steps to Health Connect, such as Google Fit, Samsung Health or Fitbit.

I walked without my phone

Use Settings → Log a walk to add steps from a treadmill, a watch that doesn't sync, or a phone-free hike.

Looking back along the road

On the Hike screen, swipe the scene (or drag the timeline) to walk back over the road you've covered. Tap You › to return.

Sound

Tap the speaker at the top left of the Hike screen. On iPhone, the silent switch mutes the app's sounds.

Starting over

Settings → Start a new journey erases your progress. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Privacy

Your data never leaves your phone. Read the privacy policy.
